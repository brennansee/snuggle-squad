Welcome to The Pillow Patch

A soft, magical world where every night ends in sweet dreams.

A magical world

Up above the clouds, past the moon and just left of the stars, floats a magical place called The Pillow Patch. It's a soft, cosy world of patchwork hills, puffy clouds and twinkling skies, and it's home to the Stuffy Boos.

Meet Peek and Boo Boo

Leading the way are two best friends. Peek is the sharp-eyed explorer, always first to spot a fear creeping out at bedtime. And Boo Boo, the original Stuffy Boo? When the fear needs to go, Boo Boo sends it running.

Together with the rest of the Stuffy Boos, they help every little one feel safe, snug and ready to drift off to sleep. When fears come out at night, the Stuffy Boos make it right.
