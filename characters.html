Meet the Stuffy Boos 🐾

When fears come out at night, the Stuffy Boos make it right. Together they help every little one feel safe, snug and ready for sleep.

  • Peek

    The sharp-eyed explorer. She spots every fear before anyone else.

  • Boo Boo

    The original Stuffy Boo. When the fear needs to go, Boo Boo sends it running.

  • Snacky Boo

    Handles every fear that shows up after dark — especially the hungry ones.

  • Jammy Boo

    Bounces fears away with unstoppable energy and the cosiest pyjamas around.

  • Blanky Boo

    Wraps every kid in warmth and safety when the night feels too big.

  • Dreamy Boo

    Tells the most magical bedtime stories until every fear drifts away.

  • Nighty Boo

    Lights up the dark and makes every shadow disappear.
